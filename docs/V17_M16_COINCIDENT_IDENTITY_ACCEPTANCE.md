## Necessary fail-closed decisions and controls

- Omit ONE of the three true transverse original FaceId contacts: strict c2 coverage and original-root Step both refuse; private geometry is not publishable.
- Forge a child/source FaceId link in an otherwise geometrically plausible real conforming cut: d2 has no authority to weld, no final cut/join candidate or retirement journal.
- Exhaust strict source seam pair work budget: no consumable original-root Step or dirty/mirror delta, and the old reader stays valid.
- Tangent/coplanar, separate cavity shells, multiple incompatible fans, source subdivision mismatch without proven refinement, wrong reflected fixed-point/self orbit, and non-manifold topology remain protected by the M03/M07/M09/M10 adversarial controls. Neither proximity nor reflection alone authorizes a weld.
- Existing M09 queued worker regression covers refusal followed by a valid command, stale root rejection, synchronized three-controller mirror history, one undo/redo and durable checkpoint. No automatic sculpt-triggered union is introduced by this milestone.

All positive results must independently check closed connected manifold edges **AND** per-vertex connected fans, correct opposite oriented edge incidences, no nonadjacent final intersections, original FaceId/VertexId provenance, exact protected original corner XYZ, and unchanged original immutable readers. Negative results must have NO terminal root, commit, partial retirement or journal. The tests classify a *supported successful union* differently from a *safe refused union request*.

**Scope remaining:** arbitrary coplanar constructive geometry, invalid imported meshes, global duplicated-coordinate cleanup, arbitrary four-sheet Boolean junctions, large stress cases and universal resource bounds are **not** proven.
