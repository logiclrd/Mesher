## Policy locked

R06's PRIMARY obligation is to **close the hole**. Start from an **already legally admissible** original cap generated by the existing fixed R03 rim / M17–M19 constructor. Keep a copy of that cap as a hard fallback. Attempt fairing on an independent working copy of its **entire new generated interior**. A valid proposed cap may be chosen only when it produces a **meaningful improvement in the whole-patch smoothness objective**, does not regress unacceptable local maxima, obeys all minimum quality, fixed rim, exact mirrored-orbit, orientation and collision tests, and the unmodified downstream source-complete PRIVATE certification. If optimization fails, times out, stagnates, or has no better legal candidate, use the **unchanged original cap**, not a new repair refusal. An original cap that was already invalid is NOT certified by a fairing fallback.

This is not M20's excise/recap recursion. No anomaly mask, purple second-hole contour, fake diagnostic stage edit or source-survivor halo is part of M21.

## Objective and constraints

- Measure source seam discontinuity from the real original-survivor normals (M18 rim profile), plus cap/cap normal variation across **indexed** shared edges. Weight normal angle squared by a bounded area/distance scale, not raw maximum absolute dihedral alone. Record whole-patch mean and worst cap/cap turn; a real source crease may remain even in an optimal surface. Preserve a hard no-regression check on seam maximum and worst interior angle (to within tiny numerical tolerance).
- The initial experiment uses **fixed topology**, applying finite, small, joint interior-vertex adjustment/relaxation and step-size search against the whole-cap objective. Exact original rim vertices are immutable; symmetry mates are transformed simultaneously by exact constructor orbits. Preserve the broad source shape with limited displacement relative to the original cap and an explicit closeness penalty.
- Each candidate undergoes triangle minimum quality (at least 0.10, and no worse than the baseline's accepted mandatory floor), orientation vs original, and the existing cap-local collision checker. A temporary local improvement is not a whole-survivor certificate. Failures are logged only; they cannot reject a baseline that was already legal.
- Future optional topology optimization can add safe diagonal flips/collapses while retaining the same **accept/fallback rule** and exact source-backed private admission.

## Safety and design distinctions

A large local dihedral by itself does not prove a ripple (M20 false positive). Smoothness should be evaluated as a *global objective* with source continuity and neighborhood guards, not an attempt to classify one exact defective face. Whole-cap averaging without shape retention can erase intended details; constrain displacement and permit no change when the evidence is weak. A re-triangulated cap can be locally legal yet fail source-complete assembly; original fallback must be maintained, not lost during a speculative private trial. Existing repair validity gates take precedence over optional fairing.
