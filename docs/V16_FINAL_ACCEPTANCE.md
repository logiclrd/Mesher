# V16 final acceptance — private repair foundation (2026-10-16)

## Decision and precise scope

**ACCEPT V16 as the tested, private, transactionally validated cap/zipper/excision foundation.** This is a *qualified subsystem acceptance*, not a claim that arbitrary collisions are solvable, automatically classified whole-triangle removal is universally correct, or live `Merge geometry` works. V17 and V18 are not enabled. This decision covers `main` at commit `75ea54fcef694de63b1383df8edce2a1c9db685e` and its completed [post-merge CI run](https://github.com/logiclrd/Mesher/actions/runs/38996720389) (Linux/GCC, Linux/Clang, Windows/MSVC; 23 CPU CTest suites). Review checked the implementation, the named fixtures and existing CI evidence; **no new interactive or performance trial is implied**.

The successful low-level V16 fixtures often invoke `planRemovalContours` with *explicitly nominated* faces. Such successes prove topology construction and private validation for those inputs, NOT that V13/V14/V15 would choose these same faces automatically during a brush Step. `planRemovalContours(...).ready()` is never sufficient evidence of burial. Classified nonmirrored deletion is instead exposed through `applyClassifiedExcisionRepair` with a caller-chosen `ClassifiedFeaturePolicy`. There is no equivalent end-to-end **classified symmetric** entry point yet.

The last two difficult-input fixtures (`testNearTangentAndFoldedClassificationStress` and `testFoldedDeepMultiShellCollisionMatrix`) establish *contact exercised and private source immutability / accepted-scar checks*. They allow conservative refusal. **They do not establish successful reconstruction of arbitrary overlapping, rotated, sheared, folded or multiply intersecting components.**

## Known refusal / unsupported subset

An unsupported geometry case must produce diagnostics and **no partial publication**: open, branching or nonmanifold boundaries; invalid projected cap polygons; highly folded or near-coplanar contact; tangency and uncertain winding; insufficient hidden-face evidence; near-tied cap/zip area scores; strongly skewed/nonplanar/unmatched contours; excessive edge, triangle-quality, vertex-fan or surface-deviation costs; too many contours, fragments, added elements or residual faces; stale plans; incomplete or inconsistent mirror maps; unclassified on-plane and near-X=0 elements; distinct reflected crossing-edge orbits meeting at one plane point; tangencies and >2 transverse contacts on one self-reflected disk ring; incoherent normalization/replan topology; potential legitimate cavities; and general deep multibody retopology beyond the tested preconditions.

Four total plane contacts **distributed across two independent valid two-contact rings** remain supported; four contacts **on one invariant disk ring** are not. Legitimately buried original material may disappear; indiscriminate two-sided preservation would be incorrect.

Some budgets bound explicit lists/insertions/sampled comparisons, **not every possible CPU path**. In particular, `RegionTopologyIndex::positiveWindingAt` can exceptionally invoke `referenceWindingAt`, which traverses the entire revision; collision discovery can also have unfavorable pathological workloads. No strict O(local), universal realtime, or total-process-RSS bound is accepted.

## V17 activation boundary — prerequisites, not accomplished by V16

1. **Explicit opt-in and default:** Keep today's unmodified sculpt behavior by default. `Merge geometry = On` is a separate V17 feature, never silently enabled because V16 primitives passed.
2. **One complete proposed Step:** Run Inflate, local split/flip/collapse, and *then* query collisions on the entire unpublished post-Step candidate. Include both simultaneously moving sheets and all relevant changed vertices/faces. Never classify against the prior published solid or against a half-finished intermediate.
3. **Deletion authorization:** A manually nominated low-level `Ready` plan is not a safe live removal policy. Define a symmetry-aware classified caller equivalent to the explicit nonmirrored policy; reject ambiguous winding, mixed/exposed loss beyond chosen tolerance, and any unhandled collision. `NoChanges` only authorizes normal publication if collision checking establishes there is no new forbidden intersection; a nonempty collision set cannot be ignored merely because the planner finds no sufficiently hidden faces. After surgery, verify the relevant entire final changed neighborhood, not just newly inserted scar triangles.
4. **Failure transaction:** If repair fails, cannot meet a budget, or residual collisions remain, use conservative *whole-Step geometry rejection* initially (no new published root and no history entry); do not publish invalid deformation or try an unproven fallback. A rejected geometry-changing Step is **accounted for and consumed in input order**, not silently dropped or retried indefinitely. Later constraint/attenuation fallback requires its own proof. Report refusal without killing the worker. `AsyncSculptWorker::run` currently catches exceptions outside its loop; a V17 per-Step recoverable-rejection path must be added.
5. **State staging:** Commit mirror-pair additions/removals, IDs/tombstones, incremental topology/BVH indices, renderer dirty owner partitions, undo/redo delta, checkpoint metadata and worker counts as one coherent change. `VersionedSculptBrush::stamp` currently adds certain refiner mirror pairs to the collapser before final root publication; under V17, rejection must roll back/stage every mutable auxiliary state change too. Intermediate normalization and scar revisions remain private; **at most one `publishIfCurrent(originalStepRoot,finalIndex)`** for the completed Step. Failed CAS leaves no auxiliary state advancement.
6. **Fidelity and overlap:** Pick feature witnesses only from preserved exposed detail, establish their original revision, spatial patch correspondence, mesh-edge scale, lifetime, reset/hysteresis and cumulative acceptance threshold. Explicitly permit buried features to vanish. Audit over an early root as well as the immediate proposed neighborhood; failures refuse the Step under the chosen policy. Neither witness mode certifies continuous Hausdorff distance.
7. **Budgets and telemetry:** Gate collision enumeration, fallback solid-angle winding, patch classification, contour DP, normalization, scar generation, residual verification and original-witness tests with bounded measured work or explicit safe refusal. Expose accepted/no-contact/rejected statuses, reason, candidate/intersection counts, preservation metrics, work and elapsed CPU time; avoid an unconstrained global winding fallback in the live worker.
8. **New V17 tests:** Repeat-tower symmetric merging with two moving sheets; clean no-contact paths; mixed-face exposed/buried cases; mirror crossing refusal; same-area repeated strokes and nonzero erosion cases; rejected-Step continuation preserving every input sample; old-reader/undo/redo/checkpoint and dirty GPU/picker ownership consistency. Verify that accepted candidate roots have no relevant residual intersections, and refused candidates do not change any published or staged state. Cross-platform warning-clean CI before feature activation.

`Merge geometry = Off` remains a distinct V18 collision-constrained displacement mode. Broad desktop Vulkan, imported-mesh and 5k/20k/100k+ interactive throughput acceptance remains V19, not retroactive evidence for V16.

## Review result and maintenance rules

**No additional V16 geometry primitive or corrective regression was identified as mandatory to accept the above private supported subset.** Acceptance relies on the named existing regression fixtures and the previously completed cross-platform CI; it does not claim new runtime testing. Future V17 integration must satisfy the activation prerequisites above, especially budgets, classified symmetric policy, post-surgery collision coverage, recoverable per-Step failure, and staged auxiliary state.

Do not change active sculpt-worker behavior in a V16 acceptance-documentation PR. If further investigation discovers a private-surgery safety bug, reopen the V16 gate with a narrowly scoped fix and regression rather than broadening its claims silently.
