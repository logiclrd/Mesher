The score is zero on planar patches but not on legitimate curved/creased ones. It does not identify rapidly alternating **signed** normal change across successive face strips or normal-field variation at local edge-length scale.
- Proposed moves are limited to finite Jacobi/vertex Laplacian moves toward one-ring mean; a normal-direction/wider surface reconstruction gradient is not currently explored. Rim vertices are fixed. These candidates can improve a global average elsewhere without correcting the specific small oscillation.
