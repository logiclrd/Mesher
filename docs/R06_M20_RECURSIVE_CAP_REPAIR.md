# R06-M20 — Iterative curvature-residual excision and local recapping

**Design snapshot:** 2026-10-25. **Status:** proposal approved for milestone staging; no automatic secondary excision or recapping authorized by the first milestone.

## Motivation and scope

R06 already reconstructs a removed contact region with **fresh** cap topology, so merely replacing existing topology is not the missing ability. On the immutable `refusal-1.r06case`, M17D/M18D/E and M19A3–A6 progressively reduced the returning hook visible in the step debugger. At the ±8 original-rim-median-edge M19A6 trial, a smaller, non-pathological ripple remains. Further brush stamps can amplify the localized abrupt normal variation. User proposes: **cap first, scan for cap curvature/normal discontinuities not explained by the original survivor set, excise the defective subset plus enough transition faces to reach a healthy boundary, and cap that smaller hole again.**

This is **iterative local corrective patch replacement**, not a claim that the base cap generator or its current finite candidate search is mathematically optimal. It is distinct from classic isotropic remeshing on an already good reference surface: unconstrained remeshing projected back to the defective first cap can reproduce the same ripple.

## Core design

1. Build original PRIVATE `SpatialCapPatch` as before (optionally including existing M17–M19 position/diagonal trials). Keep R03 contour, original survivor IDs/positions, original directional normals/curvature/confidence, and source provenance unchanged.
2. **Read-only diagnostic:** create an exact local edge/face incidence map. For each generated CAP/CAP edge, measure signed/absolute normal change and its spatial scale. Compare with an expected normal progression from the pre-repair survivor set where curvature is reliable. If not, separately label the edge as **heuristic/low confidence**, using conservative background normal variation; never pretend the regenerated cap is authoritative training data for the original curvature target. A real source crease is not automatically an anomaly.
3. Detect a connected island of abnormally high residual, then grow by a small bounded halo until the tentative boundary is less distorted. Determine the potential second hole exclusively from exact *local vertex indices and oriented face incidence*, never geometric near-vertex welding. A boundary intersecting the fixed original rim is flagged explicitly; remeshing source-survivor faces is NOT silently authorized.
4. Validate that excision would yield one or more bounded, simple, oriented contour walks without forks, nonmanifold edges, or degenerate faces. Show a diagnostic face mask plus the proposed exact contour. **Do not remove faces in M20A.**
5. Later: remove selected CAP faces in a **copy** of the PRIVATE local patch, preserving the unaffected generated faces as constraints; construct and fair a second patch from the new boundary, with original-source curvature reference retained. Splice only after exact indexed/manifold, q, normal orientation, mirror orbit, cap-local embedding and source-backed whole-candidate checks. Do not modify the worker root, checkpoints, history, or original captured `.r06case`.
6. Re-evaluate the *whole* replaced cap. Accept only if the anomalous maximum falls meaningfully, without introducing comparable new anomalies elsewhere or excessive deformation/complexity. No blind recursion: a finite iteration, face, vertex, collision-work and growth budget; stop on stagnation, unavailable trustworthy boundary, or strict-refusal and preserve the previous candidate.

A deterministic cap algorithm called with exactly the same boundary and constraints would generally regenerate the same result; a changed smaller boundary and newly healthy cap-neighbor constraints are the reason re-capping *might* help. No guaranteed success. If the defect is constrained by the original fixed R03 rim or a true original crease, a generated-faces-only secondary hole may be insufficient. Extending into original survivor faces needs a **separate** source-backed policy and certification milestone.

## Proposed score and safety considerations

For two CAP triangles sharing edge e, let observed normal turn be `theta_e`, centroid spacing `s_e` (bounded below by a fraction of original median rim-edge length), and expected directional turn from credible source curvature be `theta_expected,e`. The provisional abnormality is `max(0, theta_e-theta_expected,e)`, optionally normalized by `s_e`. Merely minimizing total normal jump would incorrectly flatten a real curved surface or erase a deliberate sharp crease. The M18B confidence-qualified shape operator may provide directional expected curvature near the original rim, but on the exact refusal-1 case only a minority of those fits were reliable: use a visibly labeled low-confidence fallback, not invented accurate tensor evidence. Avoid comparing qualified means over different eligibility masks. Curvature anomaly thresholds are **diagnostic**, separate from the unchanged q>=0.10 new-cap hard floor.

False positives to guard against: intentional creases and sharp features, inherently curved spheres/torus/hollow shapes, noisy source normals, very small triangles inflating apparent curvature, near-reflected seams, source boundaries, and non-disk topology. A large absolute dihedral is a lead, not a definitive defect. A very long normal-search range can generate locally fair but distant geometry; the anomaly classifier is not permission to accept those candidates.

## Implementation staging: independently testable, flat milestones

- [ ] **M20A1 — Pure, bounded local CAP/CAP anomaly + secondary-contour analysis.** Inputs: immutable local patch, original survivor normal/reference confidence, optional known mirror orbits. Outputs: measured edge scores and confidence, deterministic connected anomalous face island(s), bounded halo and exact proposed secondary hole contour or a documented refusal. No topology/position changes, no source fallback, no publication. Unit fixtures: flat, a localized fold, real crease, reflected/nonreflected, non-disk/fork, source-rim contact, and finite-budget failure.
- [ ] **M20A2 — Opt-in step-debug diagnostic visualization.** A default-OFF `--repair-recursive-diagnostic` flag only for interactive saved-case stepping. Distinct anomaly-face color, distinct secondary contour rods (NOT the live R03 yellow boundary), HUD counts/caveats, no mutation and preserved previous/current step replay. - [ ] **M20A3 — Exact archived regression and warning-as-error CI.** Verify original R03 contour and generated faces/positions unchanged versus equivalent non-diagnostic build; exact candidate counts/walk deterministic and bounded; GCC/Clang/Windows MSVC /W4 /WX; no worker/history/checkpoint publication.
- [ ] **M20B — Secondary excision, privately local patch only.** Create a legal open contour in an unpublished working copy; visualize before rebuilding. Never delete real survivor geometry by inference.
- [ ] **M20C — Single secondary recapping attempt.** Reuse original reference curvature/confidence and oriented contour mapping; preserve untouched generated faces, validate full local patch and original survivor assembly. Log explicit reasons for unchanged/rejected proposals. User Vulkan A/B.
- [ ] **M20D — Bounded multi-round controller and complete acceptance.** Strict merit improvement, non-increasing residual/baseline guards, finite total work, symmetric reflected atomicity, original saved cases plus healthy/crease/torus/hollow sphere and physical Linux/Windows.
- [ ] **Separate future:** optional R03-survivor halo expansion after exact source-backed permission and validator coverage; likewise live sculpt stamping integration is R07/R08 work, not entailed here.

## Architecture

Prefer a **pure local patch** analysis and eventual mutation before `MixedClosureStaging::stageProvisionalVertex` and `ensureFace`: avoid installing a temporary invalid or recapping a partially staged source index. For M20A, copy only lightweight diagnostic face identities and suggested exact edges into immutable step snapshots. This is metadata, **not** a virtual face or a stage topology mutation. Existing `MixedClosureStaging` is the single editor for later committed private transactions. Keep M16 step gate responsive (no Vulkan work in repair worker), bound frame bytes and preserve two-frame previous/current semantics. Color markers cannot be mistaken for certified closure. The M19A6 positional preview and M19D diagonal preview are separate experimental baselines; do not automatically combine them.
