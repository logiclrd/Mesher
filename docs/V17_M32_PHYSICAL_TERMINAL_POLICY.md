## Repeated explicit authorization without reanchoring

M11 uncovered an important multi-Step policy distinction. An earlier genuinely **intended** union has already displaced its source witnesses away from the FIRST original. On the next edit, a declaration that names only the NEW region would reclassify the preceding deliberately sculpted source as unscoped protected geometry and refuse even though no new damage happened to it. The solution is neither threshold inflation nor automatic proximity-based protection relaxation.

The **caller explicitly continues to nominate the previously authorized original FaceIds together with the newly sculpted region**. At each pre-CAS decision, previously named FaceIds are accepted only when they still have current M27-certified living descendants. If the complete M26 lifetime has **independently sealed whole-source Buried/Outside retirement** of a previously intended original, its now-inactive FaceId may be omitted from the active M21 declaration. A missing live source without that exact retirement proof refuses. Original source FaceIds never rebase; future unselected tower regions remain protected. This is deliberate cumulative authorization, **not a permanently inferred exemption**: omission of formerly intended but surviving faces from a later declaration restores threshold protection, as tested.
