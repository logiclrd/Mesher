## 2 → 2 flip source proof

Only an actually committed `VersionedLocalRefiner` RegionEdit emits
source evidence: the exact before/after immutable index pair, the two
unchanged permanent FaceIds, their ordered pre/post VertexId triples
and their explicit runtime vertex-mirror involution, if symmetric.

The independent auditor replays M24's split-only source evidence up
to its real last split root, then validates every flip in order. The
oriented four-edge outer boundary must be exactly equal on both sides
using permanent VertexIds (not XYZ nearest-neighbor equivalence).
Both triangles retain the same set of exactly four original source
vertices, with no position change and an actual new diagonal. All
reported changed FaceIds and transaction roots must match. When a
flip changes a protected original patch, BOTH faces must be certified
descendants of the SAME first-original source FaceId, and the total
triangle area must remain within the strict 1e-8 relative tolerance.
Protecting only one face, or two independently owned faces, refuses
rather than arbitrarily transferring some original protected detail.
Mirrored cases require source-provided reciprocal permanent-ID
mirror pairs and finite reflected source coordinates. The entire
pre/post reflected triangle fan must match its source-proven partner
with opposite winding and cyclic incidence, including invariant
fixed-plane orbits. No coordinate match can infer an otherwise absent
source or mirror ownership.

A valid unprotected flip can coexist with an unrelated original
protected feature, which retains its stable descendant FaceIds;
the complete source journal is still independently audited.

## Collapse source proof

Every successfully committed `VersionedDirectionalCollapser` records
the immutable before/after roots, source survivor and retired
VertexIds, explicit mirrored counterparts and EVERY truly retired or
rewritten FaceId. The independent auditor rederives the full changed
face halo from those two source vertices and, if paired, their mirrored
permanent IDs, verifies the two-sided original edge and the actual
midpoint (or original retained X=0 seam coordinate), then checks all
rewrites and tombstones against the real terminal root. Reflected
source IDs must remain reciprocal/exactly reflected; a missing partner
or incomplete FaceId journal refuses. Every root in a multiple-collapse
sequence must match the prior transaction's immutable result.

Without the future M21 source-authorized intentional deformation
contract, a protected original face or any of its live descendants
may NOT be deleted, rewritten or moved by collapse. Certified Buried
or Outside retirement policy also remains reserved for M21/M26; no
classification permission is guessed in M25. An unaffected protected
face survives an otherwise valid source collapse and its first-origin
identity remains unchanged. Conservative refusal returns no usable
new protected lineage and cannot publish or mutate history.

Caller-declared finite face-evidence and protected-descendant caps
refuse rather than silently dropping records; equality at the exact
budget remains valid, one-under refuses. The reader's original root,
stable ID/tombstones and COW partitions remain immutable.
