# V17-M21 — Explicit first-original intended feature scope and cavity-safe policy

**Accepted:** October 18, 2026 · [PR #73](https://github.com/logiclrd/Mesher/pull/73)

## Original requirement and independent limits

M20 has proven original FaceId → real living descendants through local
splits, conservative face flips/collapse, and complete c1/c2/c3/M03/d2
Boolean unions. M27 retains the first original seven witnesses and
shortest-edge scale over a certified lifetime. M21 adds a deliberately
**explicit caller-designated original source FaceId intent**. It allows
those named, source-proven features to deviate geometrically without
mistaking unscoped exposed outer or reversed-cavity surfaces for intended
sculpt displacement. This is a **READ-ONLY decision**: M22 will connect
it to physicalExact pre-CAS acceptance; M23 controls clean-contact
episode semantics; the original M12 integrated fidelity checkbox is
still open. Desktop Merge geometry remains default-off.

This is a policy about WHOSE first-original geometric deviation is
permitted, not a substitute for exact source/vertex/face ancestry.
A brush-radius neighbor search, a candidate's final position, or a
geometrically coincident unrelated triangle can NEVER nominate a
source for exemption. The caller (future M22 worker) must explicitly
choose the intended original FaceIds from its authentic brush action.
There is no implicit automatic inference of intent.

## Opaque predecessor-bound intent

`declareOriginalPatchIntent(before, intendedSources, originalMirrors,
maxSourceFaces)` accepts zero or more unique **live first-original
FaceIds** already nominated in the certified pre-edit lifetime.
Unknown, duplicated, retired or otherwise foreign FaceIds refuse.
The resulting opaque `OriginalPatchIntentCertificate` binds the
actual immutable predecessor `LifetimeSourceCertificate` pointer and
the canonical first-original selected FaceIds; callers cannot
manufacture the certificate or replace it with a new post-edit root.

M27 now stores the exact predecessor certificate in each accepted
successor. The sealed successor also records whether the independently
verified stage was mirrored. Thus a valid candidate must be the
**DIRECT** verified M24/M25/M26 successor of the exact predecessor
named by the declaration, with original anchors and original-root
identity unchanged. An independently created sibling branch, stale
predecessor, reconstructed post-edit lifetime, fake transition count
or attempted work-counter reset refuses.

In mirrored mode the declaration requires the immutable original
source VertexId involution. Every original face must have its complete
reciprocal original-vertex orbits, exact reflected XYZ and reverse-
winding partner FaceId with identical permanent vertices in cyclic
orientation. Intended FaceIds must be **closed under that source
involution**; a true fixed X=0 singleton remains one FaceId, not two
coincident representations. The subsequent certified reflected stage
refuses a declaration with no mirror proof. Missing one half, forged
partners, an incorrect X=0 self ID, or coincident unrelated sheet
substitution is not accepted.

## Full terminal decision, including untouched cavity

`evaluateOriginalPatchIntent(before,after,scope,tolerance,
maxDistanceTests,maxLivingDescendants)` validates both opaque
lifetime certificates and their exact predecessor link before
auditing. It runs the existing M27 **complete** source-local
seven-point original witness audit across ALL living certified
original descendants, **including intended ones**: no partial or
reduced-distance shortcut is authorized. The caller declares finite
work limits, and one under the real complete comparison count refuses.

Only after this full audited provenance check are the witnesses of
explicitly intended original FaceIds exempted from the *geometric
deviation threshold*. Every other active first-original source is
compared to the same permanently captured first-original XYZ and
original shortest-edge scale. An unintended displaced exterior
or ReversedBoundary surface with deviation above the declared
nonnegative finite original-edge threshold refuses. The decision
reports the count of actually intended original sources, tested
protected originals/witnesses, certified retired originals,
actual full distance work and maximum protected original-edge
deviation. Successful return is only a private policy finding.

A source's disappearance is NEVER licensed by caller intent:
only full c1/c2/c3 signed-winding occupancy and independently
source-certified M03 Buried/Outside retirement can eliminate all
living descendants. Retired originals are reported separately
rather than counted as preserved zero-distance patches; exposed
and ReversedBoundary cavity descendants continue to require
permanent FaceId ancestry and fidelity.

## Real positives and mandatory atomic refusals

- **Asymmetric actual brush and split:** real octahedron
  displacement plus accepted M24 source split; four explicitly
  intended original faces are exempt, four truly untouched outer
  original faces retain their exact original seven witnesses.
  Under-declaring even one moved original fails.
- **Reflected actual X=0 brush and split:** the same deliberately
  moved original top-plane feature, reflected local splitting
  and original face mirror involution; four intended original
  faces have reciprocal source proof and lower outer walls remain
  protected. Omitting the involution or one reflected half refuses.
- **Independently closed exactly coincident surfaces:** two complete
  octahedra share every world-space coordinate but no permanent
  source identity. Naming the genuinely moved sheet protects all
  twelve other originals; naming only corresponding IDs on the
  unmoved coincident sheet refuses, even though their XYZ coincide.
- **Actual unmirrored M03/c3 exact union:** complete certified
  removal of a genuinely buried original uncut base is accepted
  with **no intended displacement declarations**. The four
  independent reversed-cavity original walls remain live and
  within the unchanged original-edge witness limit; a missing
  retirement certificate and one-under full witness cap refuse.
- **Actual mirrored M03/c3 exact union:** two source-certified
  reciprocal buried original bases retire legitimately with no
  caller-inferred intent, while both four-wall reversed-cavity
  components remain intact and protected.

Additional hard refusal cases include repeated/foreign/retired
original nominations, stale or sibling predecessor certificates,
invalid mirror geometry or truncated vertex map, asymmetrically
declared reflected scope, negative/nonfinite deviation threshold,
intent hiding the wrong independently coincident sheet, lack of
certified stage ancestry, and incomplete distance budget.

## Unsupported domains still deliberately fail closed

M21 does not weaken the M25 protected-collapse contract: deleting
a protected exposed/reversed-cavity source still requires separate
proof of legitimate source retirement, or later explicit audited
policy support, not simply marking it intended. M21 cannot
reconstruct a lost original source FaceId from XYZ and it cannot
grant a Boolean weld permission. It supplies the authenticated
intended-deviation policy that M22 must enforce **before** original
CAS; it does not itself change worker FIFO, checkpoints, history,
render picking, mirror journal or desktop defaults.

Seven fixed witnesses per first-original triangle form bounded
**one-sided sampled** evidence, not a continuous two-sided Hausdorff
certificate or a universal arbitrary-imported-mesh repair algorithm.
