# R06-M23 placement provenance — replay-to-generator lookup

This is a **diagnostic-only** addition to the optional unified zipper path
(`--repair-unified-zipper`). It does not change the input contours, vertex
positions, gravity, Poisson weights, contact policy, or acceptance gates.

## Why replay steps need a mapping

The advancing-front generator creates the zipper patch **before** R06
stages individual ENSUREFACE operations. A vertex is initially a
zero-based `patch_vertex` index and subsequently becomes a very large
provisional `VertexId`. The replay HUD's `A=`/`B=`/`C=` fields
identify these provisional mesh vertex IDs, *not* patch indices.
Neither a replay step number nor XYZ-proximity can safely infer which
front operation generated a vertex (a reflected mate is a different ID).

Read the ordinary stdout/stderr log for the **same exact replay**:

1. Locate `[repair-r06-m23b-face-map]` with the same `A=`, `B=`,
   `C=` as the HUD's `LATEST ARGS: ... ENSUREFACE`. The face-map
   line reports its `patch_face`, `kind=bridge|advancing_front`,
   and `pair_a`/`pair_b` (R03 contour-pair ordinals).
2. For a non-original vertex from that triangle, search
   `[repair-r06-m23b-placement]` for `provisional=<the ID>`.
   Its `patch_vertex` is the **exact** generator position index;
   `front`, `prev`, `next` are likewise converted to provisional or
   source mesh IDs. `operation_face_start` (index in the combined bridge+AFM patch) and
   `proposal_ordinal` identify the accepted front operation and
   generated vertex within it. For exact-reflection operations, the
   reflected vertex receives its own mapping.
3. Compare `raw` (initial bisector or seam-specific AFM proposal), `tangent` (after tangent-plane
   projection), `gravity` (after the optional opposing-front
   attraction), `staged` (after seam crossing/snap), and
   `poisson` (position in the completed patch actually staged).
   `gravity_shift` and `poisson_shift` quantify those movements.
   These values are printed with 17 digits of precision.
4. `local_normal` is the actual averaged advancing-front normal used
   to form the tangent projection. `afm_support_faces` is the number
   (0–2) of **already built advancing-front triangles incident to the
   selected front edge(s)** when the proposal was made.
   `supportN_patch` and `supportN_mesh` list the actual supporting
   triangle vertex IDs in both domains. Distances are signed along that
   triangle's geometric normal:
   - `raw_dist`, `tangent_dist`, `gravity_dist` use the
     supporting triangle's position **when the proposal was made**.
   - `poisson_dist_old_plane` tests the final vertex against that
     original support plane.
   - `poisson_dist_final_plane` tests the final vertex against the
     support triangle **after** the Poisson solve.
   These reveal whether the initial projection, gravity, or later
   smoothing introduces the offset from a particular adjacent face.

A record with `afm_support_faces=0` is not evidence that no surface
exists. The original **finite-width bridge seed faces** are generated by
the zipper controller and are not part of the cap constructor's
`state.patch.faces`. That front corner relies on supplied boundary
guidance until the AFM adds supporting faces. The trace separately
reports `seed_support_faces` and `seed_supportN_mesh`/
`seed_supportN_face`, with signed raw/tangent/gravity/Poisson
distances to any matching **real seed face** (which has a fixed
boundary position). The face-map identifies these seed faces as
`kind=bridge`. A zero count for *both* kinds indicates no actual
already-created supporting face was found at the selected front edges.

On the optional M23B combined-neutral-gravity experiment, side-0
(neutral) seam-front proposals attract toward **both** origin-side 1 and 2
active vertices, excluding other neutral vertices. Side-1/side-2
proposals still use the other side only, with the unchanged softened,
mass-normalized force and 0.3*h cap. Exact self-reflected seam vertices
remain at X=0. Thus a neutral placement can now have a nonzero
`gravity_shift`; compare the new trace with the previous zero-shift
baseline to see whether it survives Poisson and improves visible curvature.
This change is still PRIVATE and opt-in and does not certify smoothness.

Only **accepted** AFM candidate vertices have placement records.
Rejected speculative trials are never mapped or printed. A vertex
introduced by the finite bridge has a face-map entry but does not have
four AFM-proposal phases. Later refinement splits can create additional
vertices that likewise have no AFM placement record; inspect their
`SPLITEDGE`/lineage event instead. The final source-certified mesh may
reassign provisional IDs to durable IDs, so for this lookup use the
PRIVATE step replay **before** final ID resolution.

## Determinism and verification

The record is associated by exact integer ID, *not* floating point
matching. Its two emitted streams are computed from the same accepted
patch and final patch-to-provisional mapping. The dedicated
`repair_r06_m23b_refusal2_new_zipper_private` test independently
checks that every traced vertex exists in staged geometry and all
face-map arguments match staged zipper triangles. This is a test of a
desirable invariant, not an assertion about the shape of today's
unwanted corner.

The measurements make no claim that a particular plane must be exactly
respected after gravity or Poisson; which stage causes the observed
bend is an empirical question. Ordinary caps and legacy zippers do
not emit these diagnostics.
