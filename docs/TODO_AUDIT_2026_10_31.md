Their full requirements remain intact in the snapshot; the new roadmap has **R06-TRIAGE** and **R07-QA** rather than 89 separate stale checkboxes.

**Evidence rule:** a closed-but-unmerged PR is *not* proof a feature shipped (for instance several R06 M06–M13 experiments in PRs #145–#161). - **R07-QA** absorbs redundant frozen/private repair acceptance checks: old R06-M06, M14F, M15D4/D, M16F, M17E, M18F, M22E, M23B3, M24A, R07D and AFM-only cleanup. These do not represent 11 separate release blockers unless the underlying behavior is independently still failing. Inspect actual live AUTO self-contact on device; preserve source-backed closure, refusal safety and history tests.
- **UX06 + UX07F + QA-UX** separate code availability from physical native-dialog support and real-file round-trip validation; avoid separate Windows/Linux/macOS checkboxes on every individual serializer.
- **V18-M07** is the single closure gate for original V18. [PR #135](https://github.com/logiclrd/Mesher/pull/135) explicitly shipped the acceptance protocol without claiming its Linux/Windows hardware observations were complete. M01–M06 remain accepted implementation; do not re-open each as a new task.
- **V19** is independent, broader robustness/performance investigation; it is not proof of unbounded behavior, exact CCD or universal imported-mesh correctness.

## Deferred research not silently discarded

The snapshot preserves optional whole-cap fairing/Poisson experiments (M21, M22D), frozen debugger-stage UX (M16D/E), other multi-contour classifier candidates (M08C), constrained collapse/flip alternatives (M11/M17), older forced-zipper/cap CLI experiments, and ambitious R08 future semantics. These need **explicit re-scoping** against the current AFM-only, always-on certified AUTO architecture before implementation. The absence of a live checkbox does **not** mean those old proposals have been implemented.
